*Last updated: 2026-10-07*

This policy applies to services purchased on our payment site, [pay.globularcluster.ca](https://pay.globularcluster.ca).

## When you can get a refund

- **Within 7 days of your first purchase**: the first payment of a subscription, or any one-time prepaid order, can be **fully refunded** within 7 days of payment — no reason needed.
- If we cannot provide a service you have paid for, we refund the undelivered portion.

## What is not refundable

- **Subscription renewal charges are not refundable.** You can cancel at any time; the subscription ends at the close of the current paid period and you will not be charged again.
- First purchases more than 7 days old.

## How to request a refund

1. Email <support@globularcluster.ca> with the email used to order, the payment date and the amount.
1. We will confirm within 2 business days.
1. Refunds go back to the **original payment method** — card payments to the card, WeChat Pay and Alipay payments to the original wallet — usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank or wallet provider.

Once refunded, the related service ends; for subscriptions, the subscription is cancelled as well.
